About Me

Pushing pixels since 2021

Designer by craft, editor by curiosity, professional by habit. I make festival posters, class promos, gaming thumbnails and short-form edits, work that has to land in a feed full of noise.

My stills start in Photoshop and Illustrator: composition, type, color. My motion runs through Premiere Pro: cuts, transitions, sound sync. And when a client needs the unglamorous stuff, like Word docs, Excel sheets and PowerPoint decks, I make those look considered too.

Honest note: video editing is my newest discipline. Everything shown here is real output, compressed for the web, full-quality files on request.

Good design isn't decoration. It's the reason someone stops scrolling.
